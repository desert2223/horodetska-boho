Information & FAQ.

Answers for visitors exploring the atelier. Commercial terms will be finalized with the designer before online orders are opened.

Can I purchase directly from this website?

Not yet. This is a design prototype, not a functioning checkout. Please use the official Instagram account for enquiries. Prices and availability shown in future catalogues will be confirmed by the atelier.

Are all garments one of a kind?

The brand emphasizes unique author-made pieces. The exact status of a given garment and whether a related version can be commissioned must be confirmed by the designer before an order.

Can I request a bespoke or similar piece?

Send the atelier a message describing your interest, preferred dimensions and location. Possibilities, time and price depend on the designer's assessment.

Do you ship internationally?

International delivery options and costs have not yet been formalized for this website. Please confirm availability, customs obligations and shipping costs with the atelier before committing to a purchase.

What is the return policy?

Final sales terms must comply with applicable consumer laws. Different rules may apply to genuinely personalized products, but a made-to-order label alone does not remove statutory consumer rights.

Are the photographs AI-enhanced?

Some concept editorial photographs may have backgrounds and lighting enhanced using AI. The website will separately retain original reference imagery for verified product listings, with careful checks of garment color and textile details.
