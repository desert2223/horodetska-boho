Our story.

An individual fashion language, rooted in Ukrainian creativity and developed through years of dedicated making.

Meet the designer

Svitlana
Horodetska.

Founded in 2016, Horodetska Boho is an independent fashion project shaped by personal expression, textured textiles and a fascination with hand-knitted details. The designer's Ukrainian roots remain an important part of the brand's identity.

Today, the creative work continues in Slovenia. This is a preliminary brand narrative; the full biographical story will be written together with Svitlana before the public launch.
